## Findings

**1. [M] The whole-frame mean understates a region-confined defect by 15-25 dB on 22 of the 42 cases.** I rendered source frames 0-5 of every case at 24 fps (CPU, lavfi) and measured the moving band as `bandmetric.py` defines it (|frame 2 - frame 4| > 12 levels, dilated 4 px) and the non-flat share (gradient > 2 levels), in `~/Developer/np-scratch/swarm-20261021/instruments/share.txt` (script `share.py` beside it). Dilution is 10 log10(1/band share), the dB a defect that lives only in the band is understated by a frame mean.

| band share | cases | dilution |
|---|---|---|
| under 3 % | 22: L0-L6, L8, L9, M3, M4, A1-A3, F2, R1, R2, O1-O4, V3 | 15.7-25.2 dB (A1 0.3 %: 25.2; L1 0.6 %: 22.5; L2 0.9 %: 20.3) |
| 3-4 % | 2: F1, P5 | 14.4, 14.8 |
| 8-11 % | 14: L7, M1, M2, O5, O6, A4-A7, R3, V1, V2, H1, H2 | 9.4-10.8 |
| 20-28 % | 4: P1-P4 | 5.5-7.0 |

[J] Worked consequence: `analyze.py`'s docstring (L26-27) calls L2_trans_16px at 71.08 dB "already far past any visible error". Over its own band that is about 51 dB. A region error of 20 dB on L1 reads as 42.5 dB on the frame, above the 40 dB cap (`analyze.py` L63, L131), so the capped mean counts it as nothing. The cap and the dilution compound: for the 22 small-mover cases the cap on the frame is a cap of 15-25 dB on the object. The dilution shifts hold, linear and the shader alike, so a per-case "vs linear" is nearly unchanged by it (see Proposal 1's prediction); what it hides is the absolute level and which cases the cap lets speak.

**2. [M]+[J] A frame-difference band is blind to the motions the cage section is about.** M3_period16_trap reads a band of 2.7 % against a texture share of 9.6 %; M4_belowgate 0.0 %; P5_stairs_along_v8_alias 3.3 % against 18.3 %; V3 2.2 %. Period-locked motion and sub-pixel drift leave frames nearly equal, so `bandmetric.py`'s band (built for real footage) would exclude the mover on exactly those cases. On the ladder the region must come from the scene's analytic coverage (`scenes.sh` already computes it as `ld(2)` in `_rect`, L136-141), never from frame differences.

**3. [R] Region and edge scoring exist and are wired to nothing the gate runs.** `bench.sh` L66-69 maps the psnr output to `-f null -`: no frame is kept, only `psnr_y` is parsed (`analyze.py` L52). `edgeerror.sh` L17-25 and `bandmetric.py` L18-20 read `realbench.sh` outputs (`ref_<s>.mkv`, `o_<label>_<s>.mkv`, odd frames of a 12->24 decimation) and nothing else. `smoke.sh` covers 12 tools (TOOLS.md L118); neither is among them. The 2026-10-21 build gate (TESTING.md L2367-end) ran smoke, the window probe, the ladder's capped mean and per-case 0.10 dB, real-footage PSNR/SSIM, timing, lockstep: no region, edge or field reading. The nearest prior to a region column is `probes/cage/cage.sh` L22-30: a static `crop` per case and a second `psnr` in the same graph, which is the right shape and costs no second render. [R] C1_cage_drift and C2 are in `scenes.sh` (L587, L591) and not in `ALL_CASES` (L454); `scenes.sh` L456-457 itself says a case not run by default rots.

**4. [R]+[M] `analyze.py`'s frame selection is exact for 24->60 and wrong for every other ratio.** `psnr`'s stats file numbers frames from 1 (measured, `instruments/n.log`: `n:1`, `n:2`, `n:3`), so `(n-1)%5 != 0` (L69) excludes 0-based outputs 0, 5, 10, which are the passthroughs at 24->60 only. `SKIP_STARTUP=5` (L43) drops outputs 1-5, so the first scored frame is output 7 (2.4 source frames in). `probes/masters/reading.py` executes `analyze.py`'s text to inherit the rule; `check.sh` runs 24->60, so it holds there. The survey already recorded that several proposed cases "would print a confidently wrong row" for this reason (NFRAME-LIMITS.md L3192-3193). [J] A second, unrecorded confound: the ladder is one second (24 source frames; `scenes.sh` `d=1`). A six-frame window is short of frames for about three source intervals at each end, so about a third of the sext's 44 scored outputs are window-edge frames (TOOLS.md L84 says the same of the quad, one frame per edge). [U] whether that is why the N-frame family's ladder margins are small; for the ladder lane.

**5. [R]+[J] The mean of dB is a geometric mean of MSE, and no column reads the worst frame.** `analyze.py` prints means (L66-72) and the capped mean; `reading.py` L19-21 adds "min past 5" for the masters tier only. Arithmetic: one frame at 15 dB among 44 at 45 dB moves the mean by 0.68 dB; averaged as MSE it would move it 15.6 dB. The per-frame mechanisms the record cares about (the cut gate firing, a tie flipping, a window edge) are invisible to a 0.10 dB gate on the mean. The 10-21 gate's "every case within 0.10 dB" (TESTING.md) could pass with one output frame wrecked on every case.

**6. [R]+[J] The ladder is 8-bit limited-range before libplacebo sees it, and chroma is scored nowhere.** Every scene ends in `format=yuv420p` (`scenes.sh` L79, L96, L141, L230...; L144-145: gray g becomes Y = 16 + g*219/255). The output goes back through `format=yuv420p` (`bench.sh` L68). `hostgap.sh` (TESTING.md L614-620) measured the consequence on the masters: the source's eight bits are the larger part of a 3-5 dB host gap on rotation, libplacebo's fp16 mixing 1-3 dB more. [J] The ceiling arithmetic: an exact-flow bilinear warp of 8-bit sources against an independently rounded 8-bit truth carries two roundings (1/12 LSB^2 each) plus blue-noise dither on the output (about 1/6): a region floor near 55-57 dB on textured content, 50-55 dB on the 16-px sines (bilinear error about 0.5 LSB at half-pixel offsets). The 65-81 dB readings are the flat majority; 81 dB is the exact-integer passthrough (TESTING.md L140-146). The ladder is gray, so psnr_u and psnr_v are constants there; on real footage `realanalyze.py` parses `psnr_y` only (L40). [U] which SSIM plane it parses. Chroma ghosting at 4:2:0 is unscored by anything.

**7. [R]+[J] The fp16 floor has two layers, and the readback layer is the coarser one below 16 px.** REPAIRS L10 (L125-129): the final pass's output is rounded to 1/2048 near 0.5, so `0.5 + f * 0.5/32` (`add_human_reading.py` L407-411, L427-430) reads to 1/32 px; acceleration at FS 2 to 1/512 px/frame^2; the tensor at FS 0.5 to 1/2048 per frame. The field texture itself is written raw (`add_human_reading.py` L158-159: `return vec4(f / HOOKED_pt, 0.0, 1.0)`), so if the SAVE textures are fp16 the field carries a storage floor of 1/64 px at 16-32 px and 1/128 at 8-16 px [U: libplacebo's hook texture format; METALPORT.md L215-217 says rgba32Float intermediates changed the jerk error 17.9 -> 18.0 %, so storage is not the jerk's limit]. Below 16 px the readback's 1/32 is the binding floor everywhere. `window_live.sh` L14-19 and TESTING.md L2439-2443 already proved the lift: whole part and fraction in separate channels read a timestamp to 0.0005, and a 0.0064 "queue error" was the instrument. `l4_coarse_fraction.py` L24 reached the same floor the other way (FS 64 failed its units check; FS 8 resolves 1/256 coarse texel). `fieldcheck.py`'s zero-level check tolerates 0.0005 encoded = 0.032 px, the floor itself.

**8. [R] The machine modes are at 1/8 resolution; the calibrated field instrument is another.** `fielddiag.py` L3-9: read_view 4/5/6 run at HOOKED.w/8; the calibrated instrument is TRI_DIAG at full resolution, which needs the private Metal CLI (`QUADDEMO`) or `fieldtier.sh`'s `diagvariant.py` on ffmpeg. `fieldcheck.py` then erodes 3 px and pools 24 px. So the field numbers in the record come from two instruments of different resolution and the same fp16 output floor (at FS 48, `fieldtier.sh`, 0.047 px).

**9. [R] Determinism is solved for the ladder's gate and open at the ceiling and in half the harnesses.** `mvk-env.sh` sets two MoltenVK switches; 40 of 42 cases repeat, all within 0.01 dB (TOOLS.md L62). What remains is libplacebo's blue-noise dither: a run one or two levels off at 62-68 dB, bit-exact only with `dithering=ordered_fixed` (MOLTENVK doc L22-26). `bench.sh` sets no dithering. [J] The 10-21 gate's L0_static -0.05 and +0.05 (TESTING.md results table) are that wander, not the build. `realbench.sh`, `visuals.sh`, `scenecheck.sh`, `prospect.sh`, `jitter.sh` do not source `mvk-env.sh` (TOOLS.md L62); the real-footage half of the build gate depends on the caller's shell.

**10. [R] The masters tier's truth is exact; its instrument is the ladder's.** `masters.py` exports rgb48le rounded half up, held to the engine to the last 16-bit level (`identity.sh`); `check.sh` then scores through `format=yuv420p` and `analyze.py`'s rule, so findings 1, 4, 5, 6 and 9 apply to it unchanged, and the host gap is the measure of what the ffmpeg instrument costs (TESTING.md L620-621: "the ffmpeg pipeline's honest number, not the shader's ceiling").

## Proposals, ranked

Count before search: the ladder is 42 cases, 44 scored outputs each (outputs 6-60 less 11 passthroughs), 3 modes.

**1. A region column beside the whole-frame one, from the analytic mask.** Mechanism: score each output frame over the mover's own coverage (the scene's `ld(2)`-type expression rendered as a third lavfi input at 60 fps, or, as a first step, `cage.sh`'s static crop per case over the trajectory's bounding box), as PSNR over masked MSE, alongside the frame figure. Prior attempt: `cage.sh` (static crop, two cases, not in the gate); `bandmetric.py` (frame-difference band, real footage, blind on M3/M4/P5 by F2). Prediction: absolute shader figures fall by the table in F1 (L2 71 -> about 51, A1 by about 25 dB); the per-case "vs linear" moves under 0.5 dB on L1-L4 (both methods' errors live in the band) and by more than 2 dB on L9_occlusion and the P series, where the blend's ghost and the warp's spread (about 48 px past a mover, `add_human_reading.py` L400-404) fall partly outside the mover; the number of cases under the 40 dB cap rises from the current count to over 30 of 42, which changes which cases the capped mean can hear. Kill test, written first: calibrate with a known defect added to the truth (CPU only, no GPU: add 8 levels inside a 100x100 patch of the truth stream): the frame column must read 10 log10(255^2 / (64 x share)) to 0.01 dB and the region column 30.07 dB exactly; the counterfactual, the same defect placed outside the mask, must leave the region column at inf. Then on real logs: if no case's capped contribution changes and no "vs linear" moves by 0.5 dB, the column adds nothing to the gate (it would still correct the absolute reading). Cost: a day's tooling; the static-crop form is a 42-entry table and no new render path; the mask form needs `bench.sh` to pipe gray frames to a numpy scorer, then 6 GPU minutes per shader to re-score, since no frames are kept. Rank 1. Must precede any new shader work: the gate's capped mean currently counts a 20 dB defect on a small mover as nothing (F1), so a shader change could pass the gate while breaking the thing the owner sees.

**2. A worst-frame column and a phase profile in `analyze.py`, and a rate-aware passthrough rule.** Mechanism: print min over scored frames (as `reading.py` already does for the masters) and the mean per output phase (five columns at 24->60); derive the passthrough set from the two rates (a frame whose time is within 1e-6 of a source frame) instead of `(n-1)%5`. Prior attempt: `reading.py`'s min; the survey's note that rate assumptions break rows. Prediction: on the 10-21 gate's logs (`np-scratch/build-gate-20261021/`) the min column shows at least one case with a worst frame more than 10 dB under its mean for the `-cut` tier (the cut gate is per frame); the phase profile shows phase 0.4/0.6 under 0.2/0.8 on the fast cases by more than 1 dB. Kill: a synthetic log with one 15 dB frame among 44 at 45 must read min 15.00, mean 44.32; the new rule at 24->60 must reproduce today's table bit for bit on the 10-21 logs; at 25->60 (period 12) the old rule must be shown to exclude non-passthrough frames. Inconclusive (no case shows a 10 dB worst frame) tips toward "the mean is enough today" and the column stays as the cheap guard. Cost: an hour, zero GPU; it reads logs that exist. Rank 2: the value is unknown only because nobody printed it. This and the rate rule are of one kind (columns in `analyze.py`); the rate rule is the guard the survey asked for and is folded here rather than ranked alone.

**3. Measure the fp16 floor, then lift it with a split encoding and no new pass.** Mechanism, measurement first (L10's cheap step, exactly): a probe shader built as `l4_coarse_fraction.py` builds its own (the tail's final pass replaced) that paints a KNOWN constant set by a `//!PARAM`, not an estimate, through the identical `0.5 + f * 0.5/32` encoding, for f = 4 + k/128, k = 0..15, rendered N:N for 6 frames each and decoded as `fieldcheck.py` decodes. Prediction: at most 5 distinct readings over the 16 inputs, on the 1/32 grid (exact fp16 values, as INSTINCT.md L48 saw). Kill: 16 distinct readings within 1/1000 px kills L10 and the fp16 stage is elsewhere. Then the lift: read_view 10 (a mode, not a pass) returning R = fract(x/2), G = fract(y/2), B = ((floor(x/2)+16) x 32 + floor(y/2)+16) / 1024, all exact in fp16 (k/1024, k < 1024), range +-32 px, resolution 1/1024 px at worst (a 32-fold lift), decoded x = 2 floor(x/2) + 2R; it fits rgb48le without alpha. Two design constraints: the final pass must sample the 1/8-res field at the cell's centre (as `l4_coarse_fraction.py` L39 does), because a bilinear blend across a whole-part boundary garbles a wrapped fraction; and the counterfactual is the same constant sweep, which must now read all 16 values within 1/1000 px. Then the estimator sweep (the recommendation on a textured box at v = 4 + k/128) measures the variational cascade's own sub-pixel floor, which the record cannot currently see below 1/32 px: the base's flow sits on 0.75m + 2n px (REPAIRS.md repair 3), so any sub-pixel accuracy is the cascade's and has never been read at its own resolution. Prior attempt: `window_live.sh`'s two-channel timestamp (TESTING.md L2439): the same lift, for a scalar. Cost: two hours of shader text, about 2 GPU minutes per sweep. Rank 3. Must precede field science (sub-pixel claims, the turning-point ambiguity, L4), not picture work.

**4. The mechanism-sensitivity matrix: `ablate.sh` as a standing instrument.** Mechanism: one generator switch off at a time from the player's High tier, the control reproducing the committed file (ablate.sh's cmp, L36-41), scored with Proposal 1's region column, reported as a 42 x 12 matrix of deltas. Count: control + 11 single ablations (ZERO_SEED, iterations 0,0,0,0, medians 0,0,0,0, the plain base, GLOBAL_SEED, COHERENCE_GATE, COARSE_ENERGY, ALIAS_CARRY, OUTLINE_ADOPT, PRINT_LATTICE, CUT_MOTION); three are nested (OUTLINE_ADOPT, PRINT_LATTICE and TRUST_GATE need ALIAS_CARRY, TOOLS.md L113-116), so the ALIAS_CARRY column is a triple ablation and must be read as such. Prior attempt: `ablate.sh` (2026-09-10, five variants, MSYS2-only, `$FFDIR/ffmpeg.exe` hard-coded, no `mvk-env.sh`; PROBES.md L16-22). What it takes: port to `FFMPEG`/`mvk-env.sh` as every tool at the top level does (two hours), extend the variant list, emit the matrix; 12 ladders at 6 GPU minutes is 72 minutes per run, so it is a weekly instrument, not a per-commit one. Prediction: ZERO_SEED off moves H1 by about -36 dB on the frame (19.28 against 55.86, `analyze.py` L30); the cascade off moves L2 and L6 up by 17-21 dB on the frame and down on the aperture series; at least one of the eleven columns moves no case by more than 1 dB region-scored, naming a switch that earns nothing on the ladder. Kill: a null variant (the control regenerated under an unused env var) must give an all-zero column within 0.01 dB, which also measures the floor; if the known ZERO_SEED and cascade moves do not reproduce, the matrix is wrong before it is read. Rank 4. Must precede any NEW switch: a switch that shows no column is cost without a mechanism.

**5. Bit-exact renders as an opt-in regression check.** Mechanism: `BENCH_DITHER=ordered_fixed` adds `dithering=ordered_fixed` to the libplacebo chain in `bench.sh`, and a `-f framemd5` sidecar; a refactor that must change nothing is then checked by identity instead of 0.10 dB. Prior: MOLTENVK doc L22-26 measured 32 of 32 bit-exact with it. Prediction: the shader under test twice gives identical md5 on 42 of 42; the L0_static +-0.05 of the 10-21 gate vanishes. Kill: md5s differ run to run with the switch set. Not the default: it moves every ceiling-bound number against the record. Cost: ten lines, 6 GPU minutes to check. Rank 5. Also: `realbench.sh` should source `mvk-env.sh` before it is next used in a gate (F9).

**6. Chroma, on real footage only.** Parse psnr_u/v and SSIM per plane in `realanalyze.py`; the ladder is gray and cannot be given chroma without new scenes. Prediction: chroma PSNR tracks luma within 2 dB on the recommendation and diverges on linear's ghosts. Kill: no divergence anywhere, and the column is noise. Cost: an hour, no new renders. Rank 6; not before shader work.

## Verdicts

Reasoned: the ladder's truth is exact and its render is reproducible, but the reading of it is a frame mean over scenes whose movers are under 3 % of the frame in 22 of 42 cases, capped at a level that on those movers is 15-25 dB, with no worst-frame column and no region column in anything the gate runs; the field instruments carry a 1/32 px readback floor that a proven encoding lifts 32-fold in a mode, not a pass. Proposals 1 and 2 (a day and an hour, 6 GPU minutes per shader to re-score) must precede new shader work because the gate as it stands can pass a change that wrecks a small mover; Proposal 3 must precede any sub-pixel field claim; Proposal 4 must precede any new switch. The estimate that most moves the story is F1, which is measured; the one most likely to be wrong is the storage-floor figure in F7, marked [U].

Gut: the instruments are honest about what they measure and silent about what they do not; the silence is where the next round's surprises are. Settle it by printing the min column on the 10-21 logs tonight; no GPU needed.
