## Proposals, ranked: the five open leads I would rank first

The brief's rule 5 asks for mechanism, prior attempt, prediction, kill test, cost and rank. For this lane the proposals are which of the record's own leads to run first; each already has its prediction in the record, and I quote it. All costs are the record's or [J].

**1. L10, the fp16 readback floor (REPAIRS).** Mechanism: the reading tail's final pass rounds to 1/2048 near 0.5, so every machine readback of a sub-pixel field carries about 1/32 px of quantisation. Prior attempt: none; found 2026-10-04, and it bit again on 2026-10-21 (the window probe's "0.9959"). Prediction: a known 0.1 px/frame translation read through read_view 4 shows steps of exactly 1/32 px; the small-flow floor (NL "Lead A", flows under 2 px reading three quarters) and ENERGY-TRANSFER 5.1's "1/32 px quantum" are partly this instrument, not the tracker. Kill test: no 1/32 px steps in the read (the floor is elsewhere); confirmed by steps at exactly 1/32; inconclusive (steps at another size) tips toward a second stage in the path. Cost: one N:N render of one scene plus a numpy read, about 2 GPU minutes. Rank 1: it is the cheapest step in the record and it sits under every sub-pixel number the field has (the small-flow floor, the rim biases, the cradle's third of a pixel).

**2. L8, the first-render nondeterminism on the Arc (REPAIRS).** Mechanism: a pass reads a persistent or storage texture before its first write, so the first render of a job reads whatever memory held. Prior attempt: step 1 run (three in a row: first differs 60 of 60, next two identical); the read-before-write audit for the Mac wander (TESTING, "What else is ruled out") found none, but it audited the plain and propagated bases, not the carry or the quad. Prediction: one pass in the carry's history or the quad's slot caches reads at frame 1 without a guard; adding the guard makes the first render identical. Kill test: every unguarded read found is also guarded by `pair_changed` (then the cause is the driver, and the Arc's single-run gating needs a warm-up permanently); confirmed by a byte-identical first render after one guard. Cost: a cloud reading session (CLOUD-LOCAL assigns it there) and one identity job on the Arc, about 15 GPU minutes. Rank 2: it threatens the one instrument the project trusts to the hundredth.

**3. The Sonic run (@4341, frames 815-833) under the trust gate (ENERGY-TRANSFER).** Mechanism unknown: the only real-footage harm (-2.4 to -3.0 dB) of a gate that passed the ladder (+0.21 capped, L7 +8.7) and the weave (+3 to +10 dB). Prior attempt: none since 2026-10-01 evening; the gate is HELD on exactly this. Prediction [J]: the frames are a fine print (Sonic's checkerboard or speed-line art) the aliasing flag opens on, where the quarter level's three minima within +-24 px include a wrong one that full resolution prefers on a hard-edged cel; a dump of the gate's decisions on those 19 frames (the `trust/decide.py` path) will show opened cells on a drawn pattern. Kill test: the opened cells are not on a pattern (then the harm is the gate's cost model, not its cue); confirmed if the loss sits on opened cells and a stricter `TRUST_ALIAS` closes it without losing the weave. Cost: one decimate-and-reconstruct of 19 frames and a cell dump, about 5 GPU minutes; a retune is a ladder run (6 min on the M5, deterministic on the Arc). Rank 3: it is the stated condition for a decision the owner delegated and then held; resolving it either ships a tier or closes a lead.

**4. L7, the 177 dead passes, with L9 beside it (REPAIRS; CLOUD-LOCAL).** Mechanism: libplacebo dispatches every pass whether or not it is read (settled from source, REPAIRS second round); the B->A chain in every two-frame shader is dispatched and unread. Prior attempt: L1/L2/L3 removed 2 + 490-gate + 2 passes with byte-identical renders; the pattern is proven. Prediction (the record's): byte-identical renders and time from a file down by about the dispatch share, up to a fifth on the base; [J] on Cadence High three of 92 passes, about 2-3%. Kill test: any differing frame, or no fall in time. Cost: the prune step is Cloud work (an hour of generator writing); the identity on the Arc and timing on the M5 about 20 GPU minutes. Rank 4: certain return, moderate size, and it closes the generators' recording of their full command (L9) in the same pass.

**5. The comb on the ladder, as section 5 asked (NFRAME-LIMITS § 5 item 2), with a slow-weave case beside it.** Mechanism: the ladder's only textured speeds are one coarse texel per frame, so the point-sampled pyramid's comb (settled on 2026-09-03 and again on 2026-09-30) is invisible to every regression gate; every gate added since leaves the comb open (NL "The weave"). Prior attempt: W1-W3 (a speed ramp) and the weave probes exist outside `ALL_CASES`; no fixed-speed comb case is in `scenes.sh`. Prediction: TEX_M1 boxes at 10 and 12 px/frame read 28-31 dB on the recommendation (section 8's numbers) and the weave box at 3 and 5 px/frame reads below linear on every tier but Standard and High (OUTLINE_ADOPT); the Linux witness reproduces both within 0.2 dB. Kill test: the new cases read above 40 dB on the recommendation (then section 8's comb was the stock base's alone and the cascade cured it, which would itself be a finding). Cost: two `scenes.sh` lines, `scenecheck.sh`, and one ladder run per shader of interest (about 6 GPU minutes each). Rank 5: it is the cheapest change that makes the regression gate see the family's best-documented failure; everything in leads 1-4 of ENERGY-TRANSFER is measured off the ladder today.

Loops of a kind: proposals 1 and 2 are both instrument checks; 4 and 5 are both harness changes; 3 is a diagnosis. Counts: L7 enumerates 177 passes in 49 files (REPAIRS' count); the comb enumeration is 2 speeds x 1 texture x 1 size, plus 2 weave speeds.
