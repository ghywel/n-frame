# Probes: one-off measurements that produced a recorded finding

Each directory holds the driver that made one measurement, with its method in the docstring and, where one
was written first, the pre-registered PREDICTION.md. They are kept because the record (NFRAME-LIMITS.md) quotes
their numbers and a number without its instrument is not reproducible. They are not regression tests and
`bench.sh` does not run them.

**2026-10-23 bounded reconstruction prospecting:** [prospect/README.md](prospect/README.md),
[prospect/RESULTS.md](prospect/RESULTS.md); `reconstruct.py` samples five local middle windows, reconstructs withheld
frames with High/linear, and keeps cuts/holds/noise/extreme changes outside the primary ranking. `review.py` supplies
contact sheets, native-size repeats and a packet-timing guard. This complements the older flow-disagreement prospector.

**2026-10-23 switch audit:** [switches/RESULTS.md](switches/RESULTS.md), `switches/audit.py` and `switches/compile.py`:
37 distinct environment controls, 63 wiring/dependency checks, 38 representative variants compiling through 2366
passes and five expected STICKY host rejections. Optional read-only Cadence graph provenance; no fresh image-quality
gate implied. Use-case/default conclusions: [SWITCH-AUDIT.md](../../SWITCH-AUDIT.md).

**2026-10-22 review supplement:** [cage/BAR-REVIEW.md](cage/BAR-REVIEW.md), reproduced by
`cage/bar_review.py` (CPU, numpy; output `$NP_SCRATCH/bar-review/results.json`): 13/13 controls; a correct +6 px/frame
seed on period-9 bars becomes -3 under BAR_REDECIDE, the fixed-grid interpolation response checked against direct
samples, and the rotating bars' two-dimensional alias and coarse-candidate interpretation audited.
`cage/bar_gpu.py` then runs the actual GLSL pass with exact input flow: the M5 confirms +6 -> -3 and -6 -> +3,
with +3 retained, eight instrument checks passing and the repeat byte-identical. This isolates the final decision;
it is not a paired full-High picture regression. GPU data: `$NP_SCRATCH/bar-review/gpu/`.
The sequel `cage/bar_pipeline.py` supplies that paired pipeline check (BAR-REVIEW.md section 6): a fixed guided
period-9 signal has correct +6 flow changed to -3 at 1.1587% of matched interior samples; both baselines and variants
repeat. A flow-selected region loses picture quality even while the crop gains 8.81 dB. Four cases, no exact-flow
injection; the broad-guide mechanism is still a hypothesis. Data: `$NP_SCRATCH/bar-pipeline/`.
The batched follow-up is `cage/bar_guard.py`, `guard_followup.py`, `guard_report.py`: a diagnostic broader-support
guard, a C1/C2/C3 and 45-case screen, and the frozen/headline summaries, with fixed local-error masks. The variant
and its limits are recorded in BAR-REVIEW.md; data `$NP_SCRATCH/bar-guard/`.
Uncertainty follow-ups `uncertainty/link_covariance.py` and `anchor_gls.py` use Local's delivered link arrays:
reconstructed fit p99 <.003 px/frame, frozen covariance transfer refuted on O9, and exact-anchor GLS weights also
refuted (MATHS.md blocks 3-4). They retain counterfactuals and refusal counts; no sigma or shader adoption.
Local's follow-ups (2026-10-23): `cage/bar_basin.py` builds BAR_BASIN (the guard comparing basin with basin at their
own fine minima) and runs its registered test against High, the plain pass and GPT's guard (guided scene, isolated
seeds, C1-C4); `cage/bar_draw.py` six seeded random bar prints (periods 6-12, speeds to +19). NFRAME-LIMITS.md,
"BAR_BASIN"; data `$NP_SCRATCH/bar-basin/`, `bar-draw/`, `basin-20261023/`. `uncertainty/link_regimes.py` tests the
frozen covariance on a fresh Arc export of seven cases, two sealed for a later model (MATHS.md block 5).
`content/content.py` (2026-10-23): the Cadence tiers and their switches by content type, live action, 2D animation
and CG, on a seeded draw of twenty titles from the NAS pool (NFRAME-LIMITS.md, "The tiers by content type").
`photometric/` (2026-10-23): flowtap.py (High's final flow at one pair, calibrated by a known pan), withheld.py (one
withheld frame scored per shader), fade.py and mkfade.sh (still pictures under a pure fade); PLAN.md, the defect and
its two planned fixes with their pre-registered tests.

Conventions: the repo root is derived from the script's own location. ffmpeg comes from `FFMPEG` and `FFPROBE`
(the newer probes default to `$HOME/np-build/ffmpeg/ffmpeg`, the build `../../build-macos.sh` makes; the python
scorers to `ffmpeg` on `PATH`), python from `PY` or `PYTHON`. The data they read and write -- renders, logs,
tables, generated shader variants -- lives OUTSIDE the tree under `NP_SCRATCH` (default: `np-scratch` beside the
repository checkout), one directory per topic, named below; the energy/, weave/ and mvk/ drivers take their work
directory as the first argument. A probe run from a machine without that data renders it afresh; one that only
scores expects it to be there.

**MSYS2-only.** The shell drivers written 2026-09-08 to 09-10 -- `ablate/*.sh`, `anchor/*.sh`, `cost/timing.sh`,
`jerk/jerksweep.sh`, `median/*.sh`, `shear/*.sh`, `snap/snap.sh`, `verbs/rolling.sh` -- run only in the Windows
build's MSYS2 shell. They call `$FFDIR/ffmpeg.exe` (`FFDIR` default `$HOME/np-build/ffmpeg`) with
`/c/msys64/mingw64/bin` on `PATH`, most call `python` rather than `python3`, and their `NP_SCRATCH` default is
`/e/nframe-project/np-scratch`. They are kept as they ran; on macOS or Linux, change those lines before running
one. Their python scorers (`anchor/phaseprobe.py`, `snap/snapcheck.py`, ...) are portable. None of these drivers
sources `tests/mvk-env.sh`.
