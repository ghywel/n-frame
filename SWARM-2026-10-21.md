# The first swarm: fresh eyes on the shaders (2026-10-21)

Gareth's ask, that evening: "we did a great deal of hard work on our interpolation shaders, which are truly innovative
compared to what was on the market. But i am sure there is more we can do to improve and optimise them ... it would be
the first time a fresh look would have been passed to a swarm, and gives us a great deal of scope for fresh eyes. I am
thinking in particular the synthetic tests, many of which would have been developed early and will be poorly optimised,
and may even not be brilliant candidates for the test they are supposed to represent."

Nine lanes, each a fresh reader (Claude Fable 5.1) of one slice of the record, ran in parallel for about twenty
minutes each under one brief ([swarm/2026-10-21/BRIEF.md](swarm/2026-10-21/BRIEF.md)): paper only, no GPU, nothing
written into the repository, every claim tagged read / judgement / unchecked, every proposal with a prediction, a kill
test written first, and a cost. A ninth lane read only the lay documents and brought mechanisms from other fields, the
project's random-chaos rule. The lane reports are in [swarm/2026-10-21/](swarm/2026-10-21/); the judge's notes, written
as each landed, are [swarm/2026-10-21/JUDGE-notes.md](swarm/2026-10-21/JUDGE-notes.md). About 2.1 million tokens in
all. This page is the contraction: what changes the story, what is queued to measure, and what is Gareth's to decide.

## What changes the story

Six findings, each measured by its lane on CPU from the scene expressions, the GLSL or tonight's own gate logs, and the
two that change headlines re-checked by the judge before this page was written.

1. **Three ladder cases have been scoring against a wall built into their own truth.** V2, V3 and H2 use
   `gt(sin(..),0)`, a hard threshold evaluated at integer rows, so at the 60 fps truth frames their bar edges snap to
   whole rows while any correct warp of the 24 fps frame gives partial rows. The lane's model puts the ceiling at
   30.66 dB (25.5 to 37.4 by phase); the judge's independent model at 28.6 (24.1 to 34.0); a soft texture under the
   same warp scores 52.7. The recommendation reads V2 29.02 and H2 28.64: on the wall. Every V2, V3 and H2 delta in the
   record since 2026-09-06, including the alias carry's "V3 +6", was compressed against it. `scenecheck.sh` cannot see
   it because the coincident frames are integer. The P-series is clean. (ladder-audit, finding 2; JUDGE-notes.)
2. **The last two scored frames of every ladder case are the built-in mixer's, scored under the shader's name.**
   The window's end falls through to libplacebo's mixer (the "two boundary hook-skipped lines" the benches allow), and
   `analyze.py` has a start skip but no end skip: frames 59 and 60 read identically for the shader, hold and linear on
   every case (L1: 36.63 and 31.30). L1's mean is 65.69 as scored and 67.20 without them; L2 50.12 and 51.05. Every
   shader is diluted toward hold by about a decibel on the clean cases. (mathematics, finding 8; confirmed by the judge
   from the 2026-10-21 gate logs.)
3. **Whole-frame PSNR scores the ladder by object size.** A 100 x 100 mover gets a 19.6 dB bonus over its own error, a
   300 x 300 one 10.1 dB, so the 40 dB cap is 20 dB on-object for the small boxes and 30 for the large; 22 of 42 cases
   confine their motion to under 3% of the frame (15 to 25 dB of dilution). Dividing the bonus out of the saved logs,
   the recommendation's binding set goes from 8 cases to 29 and the ranking of the three shipped tiers flips (whole
   frame: High 38.33 > rec 38.05 > quad 37.80; on-object: High 32.54 > quad 32.21 > rec 31.52). The region and edge
   instruments that would score this honestly (`edgeerror.sh`, `bandmetric.py`, `cage.sh`'s crop column) exist and
   feed nothing the gate runs. Both lanes call the re-ranking suspicious until a crop-scored render confirms it; that
   render is in the queue. (instruments, findings 1 and 3; ladder-audit, finding 3.)
4. **The shaders are not time-symmetric by output phase.** On L1 the recommendation reads 70.95 / 64.10 / 70.36 /
   63.32 dB at t = 0.4 / 0.8 / 0.2 / 0.6; on L2 51.24 / 43.32 / 66.33 / 44.06; linear is symmetric to 0.01. Every
   ladder mean is an average of exact and lattice-error phases. The mechanism is conjectured (REPAIRS L4's lattice
   fraction against the quarter-texel flow read), not shown. (mathematics, finding 1; confirmed by the judge on the
   recommendation's logs.)
5. **One gate decides every switch after the cage, and it cannot see a 9 px print.** `ALIAS_E_M_ST`'s ridge test needs
   the rival basin at least two 1/8 texels (16 px) away, so the cage, the railing and C3 can never be flagged: C1's
   rectangle flags 0.2 to 0.7% of its cells, C3's disc 1.6 to 7.8%, V3's patch 90 to 97%. C3 has been untouched by
   construction since 2026-09-21, and the shipped tiers were never scored on C1, C2, C3 or the railing. C3 at the 1/8
   level is a confident lock one period off (the weave's anatomy), not V3's tie, so a tie-breaker is the wrong tool.
   (periodic-alias, findings 2 and 3; CPU measurement with `ambiguity.py` restricted to the region.)
6. **REPAIRS L7 undercounted the dead passes by six to eight times, and its tool would have cut two live ones.**
   `dead_passes.py` follows `//!SAVE` and `//!BIND` only; the project's cross-pass channel is storage images, so it
   misses the chain behind a bound-but-dead median and calls `ALIAS_E_AB` and `ALIAS_Q_AB` dead when the live 1/8
   refines read what they store. A dataflow liveness over 950 passes in 13 files: at read_view 0 the recommendation
   runs 17 dead whole passes of 52 (35% of dispatches), the player's High tier 17 of 92, Standard 24 of 80, the base 6
   of 22, the N-frame shaders none; 14 to 15 of the dead passes recompute every output frame. The reading tail costs
   nothing at read_view 0 (libplacebo drops `//!WHEN` passes before compiling). (cost; `prune_sim.py` in its folder.)

Two more of the lanes' corrections are to the record's own words rather than its numbers. The 2026-09-10 ruling that
the family is not rebuilt, with its three measured reasons, is in Claude's memory and in no repository document; a
fresh reader would propose a rebuild (record-map, finding 1). And the record contradicts itself on whether the
per-level trust gate was built: two status headers say never, the 2026-10-01 evening entries say built, gated and
HELD (record-map, finding 3). Both are repaired below.

## Where the fresh eyes agreed

Three readers who could not see each other's work converged: the instruments lane from the scoring side, the
ladder lane from the truth side and the real-content lane from the footage side all arrive at the same instrument
(a region-scored column beside the whole-frame mean, a worst-frame column, a per-phase profile). The wildcard and
periodic lanes, from an outsider's toy and from the GLSL, split the weave's comb into the same two mechanisms: the
sampler comb (both frames point-decimated on one fixed grid, cured by comparing different samples, not by changing
their content, which is the refuted prefilter) and the print's own period ambiguity, irreducible locally. The judge
answered the wildcard's one factual question from the base shader: `LUMA_A_S` and `LUMA_B_S` are each one point
sample per texel and the coarse SAD reads B from that stored level, so the phase-exact fetch is untried. The reach
lane found the 24 px cliff is a constant (0.75 x 1.9375 coarse texels = 23.25 px) opened at the 1/16 descent and
sealed by the 1/8 prior centred on zero, the prior being the larger term in its emulation; and the record-map lane's
top five leads (L10, L8, the Sonic run under the trust gate, L7, the comb on the ladder) are the other lanes' top
leads, found independently.

## What the swarm cost and what it was worth

Nine lanes, about 2.1 million tokens, no GPU. Against that: two scoring defects in the ladder's own instrument that
have shaped every number since the reset; a truth-quantisation ceiling on the three cases the alias work was judged
on; a gate that excludes by construction the defect class two of Gareth's film reports were about; a liveness tool
that would have cut live passes; and a map of the record that did not exist. Gareth's instinct about the early
synthetic cases was right, and the swarm found it in the first twenty minutes.
